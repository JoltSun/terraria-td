Loadout Optimizer

Finds the best gear for the unit in the Build panel (same unit, tier, level and reforge). DPS is scored against the act picked in Act analysis, or the enemy in Enemy Select if no act is picked. Range doesn't change DPS in the model, so use Min RNG or a range goal to protect coverage.

Team Builder

5 tower slots. "Set from Build" copies the Build panel into a slot; "Edit" loads a slot back into the Build panel. Each slot has its own upgrade tier and number of placements. Merchant and Star Merchant are treated as economy/support and excluded from combat DPS.

DPS vs. Enemy Defense

Set a DEF value to see the attacking Team's live DPS. The graph shows every attacking tower across the full defense range.

Mana Planner

Plans every placement and individual upgrade needed to reach your Team targets, ordered by output per mana. Starting mana highlights what you can afford immediately. Combat towers contribute Team DPS; Merchant and Star Merchant contribute Mana/s instead. DPS is scored against the act picked in Act Analysis, or the enemy in Enemy Select.

Act Analysis

Enemy counts are from one recorded run per act on the Trello board, so treat them as typical, not exact. Team Act DPS uses attacking towers against each enemy's DEF and type tags, weighted by how many spawn. HP-dependent effects remain approximate because enemy HP scales by wave.

Compare Towers

Raw tower stats you can check against the in-game stat screen: no scores, no tiers, no estimates. Level 45. Click a column to sort. Best gear picks the gear with the highest stat-screen DPS.

Fantasy Teams

A fantasy 5-tower builder, as if you owned every unit and item in the game. Uses your Account Buffs and Achievements, and assumes Level 45 and the best reforge.

Slot roles: optional range band per slot (e.g., one 40+ sniper, two 15–25 midrange). A blank slot has no RNG floor.

Underrated: burn/frostburn ticks, ricochet, knockback, and stuns. The model doesn't account for these!

Account Buffs

Your whip works like in-game: equip one and it stays on for every match, affecting every tower. Banners have their own panel below. Boss Slayer and Pack a Punch tiers live in the Achievements tab. Saved in this browser.

Banners

Click a banner to mark it owned (green dot). ⚔ DPS banners change the damage math; the rest are tracked for your collection.

Achievements

Click a tier pip to set how far you've gotten (click the current one again to step back). ⚔ marks buffs the damage model uses; the rest are tracked for account planning. Saved in this browser.
